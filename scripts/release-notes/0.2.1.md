## New

- If Side quits unexpectedly, the next launch offers to report it: a GitHub issue opens in your browser with a summary of the crash (versions and where Side stopped, nothing from your files) for you to read and send.

## Fixes

- Side no longer crashes with the minimap turned off, or when Review switches to a track with fewer checkpoints.
- Line numbers follow the file you switch to, instead of keeping the last file's until the window was resized.
- A tab being dragged casts a shadow that fits it, on the tab bar and out of the window.
